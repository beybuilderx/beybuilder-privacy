Privacy Policy for BeyBuilder X

Last updated: May 2026

This privacy policy applies to the BeyBuilder X mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms below.

1. Who runs this App

This App is developed and operated by an independent developer (the "Developer"). For privacy questions, contact: beybuilderx@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Data you create in the App — saved combos, owned parts list, and similar in-app records are stored locally on your device only. They are never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising data — The App uses Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Advertising ID (for serving relevant ads and limiting ad frequency)
  • IP address (approximate, for region targeting)
  • Device type, OS version
  • App interaction events

Crash and performance data — The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect bugs. When the App crashes, it sends to Firebase:

  • Crash stack trace
  • Device model and OS version
  • Anonymous installation ID

No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number) are collected.

Cloud sync (optional) — If you choose to sign in with your Google account, the App uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to back up your saved combos, owned parts list, and battle log to Google servers. This is opt-in; without signing in, no app data leaves your device. Stored data: your Google email, anonymous user ID, and the in-app records described above.

Push notifications — The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications when new Beyblade X parts are added. No personal data is collected.

Purchases — Premium purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment information.

3. How We Use the Data

  • Ads: Serve relevant ads via AdMob to support free use of the App
  • Crash data: Fix bugs and improve stability
  • Push notifications: Alert users when new Beyblade X products are added
  • Purchases: Confirm and unlock premium features

We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it for profiling beyond what AdMob does for ad personalization.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is designed for Beyblade X fans of all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your Rights

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
  • Stop crash reporting: Uninstall the App
  • Stop push notifications: Android Settings → Apps → BeyBuilder X → Notifications → Off
  • Delete local data: Uninstall the App. All saved combos and owned parts will be removed.

7. Data Retention

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Crash reports and AdMob data are retained per Google's policies (typically 14 months for AdMob, indefinitely for Crashlytics).

8. Security

We use industry-standard security practices via Firebase and Google Play infrastructure. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or via push notification.
